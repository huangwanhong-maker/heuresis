# Release Notes: 18 November 2026

## 2. Proposals with Their Parents

A statement, a program or any genotype proposed from outside can now say which members it was made from ([decision 0007](../decisions/0007-proposals-with-their-parents.md)). A person's or a language model's variation then keeps its place in the genealogy, inherits its parents' age, and credits them when it survives, as a child of the evolution's own operators would. The infrastructure's search for needs uses it to bring a model's variations into the lineage.

## 1. An Evolution Assembled by Stage

heuresis has a new module, `evolution` ([decision 0006](../decisions/0006-an-evolution-assembled-by-stage.md)): one evolutionary loop over any genotype, put together from a choice at every stage, so that a whole algorithm can be stated as data.
- **Every stage has its strategies.**
  - Parents: tournament, ranking, Boltzmann, truncation, lexicase and more.
  - Survival: generational, (μ+λ), (μ,λ), steady state, aging, crowding, restricted tournaments, clearing, NSGA-II, ALPS, MAP-Elites niches.
  - Operators are chosen by fixed weights, probability matching, adaptive pursuit, UCB1 or Thompson sampling, each credited for what its children do.
  - Novelty may be added as an objective.
- **An archive that keeps and forgets.** Members are admitted by the caller's judge and take part as parents. They are retired, with the reason kept, when they have produced nothing that survives for a while, when they are too old, when a recheck no longer upholds them, or when the archive is full.
- **Proposals from outside,** from a person or a language model, enter a generation and are credited to their source.
- **Nothing is evaluated twice;** every child's lineage is recorded; and a run can be saved and resumed exactly.
